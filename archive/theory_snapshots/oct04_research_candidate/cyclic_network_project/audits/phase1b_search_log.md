Search date/cutoff: 2026-09-24. This log records representative executed query strings and citation paths, not a claim that a search engine exhaustively indexed the literature. A failed result was not counted as absence. Primary sources support the audit's overlap conclusions; discovery sites supplied leads only, except clearly identified indexed text from an author's uploaded version, which was not used to resolve equation identity.

The six attached papers and the Phase 1 source/version records were retained. English/Chinese searches were supplemented by DOI, exact-title, author and equation-specific queries. Some primary PDFs were available through indexed text even when direct rendering returned an empty response. Those access levels remain explicit.

## Backward citation paths pursued

- **Lin–Han 2020, p.781:** Feng–Niu–Wang 2003; Bellavite 1998; Kim 2014; Wang 2008 thesis; Ding–Wang 2008. The retrieved Ding–Wang publisher bibliography further identifies Li 1987 and Zhang–Han 1998. Missing full texts are listed individually in `additional_sources_needed.md`.
- **Lin–Han 2024, references 37–39:** enzymatic 2021 → topology comparison 2022 → multi-node comparison 2023; references 7/9 lead to Qiao 2019 and Ma 2009. Ma's primary Eq.(7) was inspected. Qiao repository access was challenged.
- **Zhang–Xia–Wang 2020, p.120504-11:** reference 11 points to Yang et al. 1995. Equation-level primary indexed evidence is available for the 2020 paper, but the older item's title remains missing.
- **Stewart 2024 bibliography:** Filipski–Golubitsky 2010 full theorem retrieved; earlier Golubitsky–Stewart, Alexander, Collins–Stewart and Ermentrout works identified as the historical frontier. No claim to have fully audited each older work.
- **Kerin–Engler and van Kekem–Sterk:** cross-checked the generic quadratic reduction against the cubic Fourier calculation in Pais; located Kerin–Engler's published DCDS-B record. Did not transplant Lorenz–96's energy hypotheses into DM.
- **Benzi–Razouk:** followed the inverse-decay lineage through Demko–Moss–Smith and related matrix-function references. Final ETNA theorem provides adequate accessible adverse evidence even while Demko's full text remains incomplete.
- **Qiu et al. 1995:** identified the Hinrichsen–Pritchard stability-radius lineage and the distinction between full uncertainty blocks and specific coefficient structures. Older full-text historical attribution remains a retrieval task.
- **Jaramillo / Pais:** the inspected Fourier and normal-form passages were compared directly. Their source models remain distinct from DM; shared machinery is not counted as an identical model.
- **Wei / Nian–Li:** retained as motivation/method boundary cases. Their observer-control or fitted chaos narratives did not justify treating a five-dimensional system as a cyclic scalar network; older five-element citations were pursued through the more directly relevant Lin–Han/Ding–Wang lineage.

## Source-access quality notes

- Final Benzi–Razouk PDF: Theorem **3.4**, p.24; the earlier institutional report labels the analogous theorem **4.1**, p.10. The audit cites the published numbering.
- S20 arXiv v1 has a generated title-page date inconsistent with its 2008 submission; the cited journal year is 2009. Locators use PDF pages, not journal pages.
- S24 is a very recent **preprint**, not independently verified; it is included because its explicit circulant theorem and perturbation formulas bear directly on the proposed novelty narrative.
- A ResearchGate page with a 2022 title showing a 2024 uncorrected proof is a version-discovery warning, not sufficient equation evidence.
- The primary DOI for Jiang–Yuan–Xiao 2007 failed to open. Its secondary metadata is a retrieval lead only.
- The Arcak–Sontag author PDF failed to open. No secant theorem is applied or asserted to subsume DM.
- Newly found clinical/data-oriented or conceptual five-element papers were screened by available model descriptions only; no clinical validity or mathematical priority conclusion is drawn from their abstracts.

The expanded search supports downgrading generic claims. It does **not** close the exact-model priority question, and it cannot guarantee that every relevant paper has been found. The remaining citations form a finite retrieval queue, not an unreported claim of exhaustive backward coverage.
