\section{Adversarial mathematical audit template}
\begin{description}[style=nextline]
\item[Audit ID, reviewer, date and independence:] Record whether the reviewer
is independent of the derivation and which version or hash is reviewed.
\item[Statement being audited:] Exact theorem text, claim registry IDs,
proof-file path and statement label. Do not audit a paraphrase.
\item[Exact assumptions:] Every quantified domain, parameter restriction,
regularity hypothesis, branch, norm, symmetry, exclusion and nondegeneracy.
\item[Dependency verification:] List each input theorem, exact version,
VERIFIED/AUDITED status and applicability; identify circular reasoning.
\item[Line-by-line proof checks:] For each step record justification, algebra,
signs, quantifiers, logical implication, and missing hypotheses. Require
independent reconstruction of critical calculations.
\item[Edge cases:] Zero and repeated roots; parameter boundaries; even/odd n;
q=1, q=n-1 and q=n/2 when applicable; gcd cases; coincident shifts;
self-conjugate modes; ties and resonances; singular denominators; state boundary.
\item[Counterexample search:] Challenge the strongest quantifier; try excluded
and limiting cases; distinguish a successful proof from failure to find a
counterexample. Record whether the search was analytic or computational.
\item[Symbolic checks:] Independent derivatives, identities, normalization,
adjoints and remainders; source of any computer-algebra expression.
\item[Numerical checks:] Independent implementation, residuals, conditioning,
precision and convergence; whether ordinary or rigorously validated numerics;
which analytic assertion the check can and cannot support.
\item[Literature overlap:] Exact source and location, hypotheses, model
dictionary, novelty limitations and any result imported by citation.
\item[Verdict:] Select exactly one: \textbf{verified / revision needed / false /
unresolved}. Explain the decisive evidence and remaining obligations.
\item[Disposition and sign-off:] Corrections, owner, downstream invalidations,
recheck version and date; do not mark AUDITED until the verdict is verified.
\end{description}
