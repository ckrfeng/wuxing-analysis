## Retrieval and clearance protocol

For every obtained paper record the exact version, title page, equation numbers, parameter and state domains, and theorem assumptions. Compare laws under explicit relabeling, time/state scaling and coefficient restrictions; distinguish a genuinely identical system from shared motifs. Separate author-proved results, numerical examples and interpretation. Search newly relevant references backward, preserving the same access labels.

Do not clear a source by title, abstract, citation count or a nonmatching first equation. Conversely, do not claim it subsumes DM merely because both involve saturation or five nodes. A priority claim is releasable only after the relevant exact comparison is documented; a claim of mathematical significance still requires independent results later.
