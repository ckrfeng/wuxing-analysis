\section{Literature audit template}
\label{sec:literature-template}
Copy this form for each source in a future audit. Keep an unfilled field
unresolved; do not infer absence of overlap from absence of evidence.
\begin{description}[style=nextline]
\item[Record ID, reviewer, date and version:] \mbox{}\par\vspace{1em}
\item[Source:] Full authors, title, year, venue, DOI or stable URL; primary
source file and provenance; verification of bibliographic metadata.
\item[Exact model:] Transcribed equations, parameter domain, normalization,
node and edge conventions, boundary assumptions; source transcription check.
\item[Theorem or numerical result:] Exact statement and evidence type;
separate a proved theorem from a simulation or interpretation.
\item[Assumptions:] All stated and implicit hypotheses, exclusions,
nondegeneracy conditions, parameter restrictions and computational settings.
\item[Page/equation/theorem reference:] Exact location in the consulted source,
including version and supplement where relevant.
\item[Relation to our work:] Corresponding registry IDs and proof modules;
explicit dictionary between equations, parameters and indexing conventions.
\item[Possible overlap:] Matching conclusions, proof methods or model classes;
whether a specialization or direct corollary would cover our proposed result.
\item[Confirmed novelty:] Leave empty until comparison is complete; attach
evidence and reviewer sign-off for a narrowly stated novelty claim.
\item[Unresolved concern:] Missing hypotheses, ambiguous attribution,
unavailable source, inconsistent formula or unreproduced numerical claim.
\item[Disposition:] Unresolved / source verified / overlap confirmed /
qualified distinction documented. Record downstream claim and scope changes.
\end{description}
