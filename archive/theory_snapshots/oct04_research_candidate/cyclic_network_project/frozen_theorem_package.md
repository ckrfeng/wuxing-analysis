Effective research scope: 2026-09-24. This adopts one planning package, not mathematical conclusions or permission to begin proofs in this phase. Keep the canonical DM and MB models and all parameter/index conventions unchanged. Keep arbitrary (n,q) for the model and supporting spectrum; focus the substantive selection and comparison program on q=2. The n=5,q=2 case is the motivating application within the same paper.

## Comparison and statement conventions

- Freeze the **same coefficient tuple** (a,k,c,g,h), same n,q and original time variable for the primary DM–MB comparison. Each model uses its own explicitly named positive synchronous branch. Compare only where both chosen branches exist; do not select a branch after seeing the desired effect. This resolves the recommended working calibration B05 for this package and supersedes its older undecided wording.
- Do not claim topology/parameter-count matching gives equal equilibrium levels, identical effective derivative signs, or equal time scales. Any additional calibration is outside this frozen comparison.
- P02 must identify the branches. P11 must state one physical crossing parameter and a regular positive branch before a first-Hopf assertion is formulated. This audit cannot choose a branch/path or a sign region on the basis of an unproved blueprint. These are precise-statement gates, not reasons for another literature survey.
- The attempted selection classification includes all admissible cases it claims, including ties, endpoints and real modes. A tie of two distinct conjugate pairs is excluded from a simple-Hopf theorem. No codimension-two unfolding is part of this paper.
- The l1 target is a certified nonempty parameter region, with the n/mode range stated exactly. A formula, a decimal sign check, or an unevaluated inequality does not meet the core sign target. If this target fails, record the obstruction and revise the scope explicitly; do not replace it silently with optional sensitivity results.
- Retain the existing Fourier normalization; settle the Taylor factorial and Hermitian-pairing conventions before P13. Make no physiological validation claim from a five-node dynamical example.
