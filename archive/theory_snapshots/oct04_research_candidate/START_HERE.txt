  literature_audit_compendium.pdf   Project literature audit.

project records, and audit/support files are included.

These additions were checked in the verification task but have NOT received
an independent A08 audit. Original manifests, historical hashes and audit
statements describe the earlier baseline; they are retained as historical
records, not certifications of the P08 extension. Package file hashes are
in PACKAGE_SHA256.json. P12 remains TODO.
